# Salesforce Technical Architect — 12-Month Master Roadmap

> **Goal:** Complete all certifications required for the Salesforce Certified Technical Architect (CTA) path, with deep focus on Data & AI  
> **Pace:** ~24 hrs/week study time  
> **Last Updated:** August 2026

---

## Full Certification Map

```
CTA (Board Review) ← PINNACLE
├── Application Architect (auto-awarded bundle)
│   ├── ✅ Platform App Builder          ← Month 3
│   ├── ✅ Platform Developer I          ← Month 4
│   ├── ✅ Data Architect                ← Month 9
│   └── ✅ Sharing & Visibility Architect ← Month 9
└── System Architect (auto-awarded bundle)
    ├── ✅ Integration Architect          ← Month 10
    ├── ✅ Dev Lifecycle & Deployment Architect ← Month 10
    ├── ✅ Identity & Access Management Designer ← Month 11
    └── ✅ Mobile Solutions Designer     ← Month 11
```

**Supporting certifications:**
- ✅ AI Associate — Month 1
- ✅ Administrator — Month 2
- ✅ JavaScript Developer I — Month 5
- ✅ Data Cloud Consultant — Month 5–6
- ✅ Agentforce Specialist — Month 6–7
- ✅ Platform Developer II — Month 7
- ✅ Advanced Administrator — Month 8

**Total: 16 certifications + 2 bundle credentials in 12 months**

**Focus:** AI fundamentals, LLMs, Einstein Trust Layer, generative AI ethics, Salesforce AI capabilities  
**Trailhead:** Agentblazer Champion level + AI Associate cert prep trailmix  
**Labs:** Einstein Prediction Builder, Prompt Builder intro, Next Best Action setup  
**Weekly hours:** 24 hrs

|------|-------|

| 2 | Ethical AI, Salesforce Trust Layer, data for AI |
| 3 | Einstein Copilot basics, Prompt Builder intro, Data Cloud basics |
| 4 | Practice exam, review weak areas, sit AI Associate exam |

| 4 | Mobile: SmartStore offline, MobileSync, sit both exams → System Architect awarded |

### Month 12 — CTA Board Preparation
**Goal:** All 8 component certs done + both bundles awarded → begin CTA process  
**Activities:**
- Enroll in CTA-601 virtual workshop (register by Month 10)
- Complete FlowRepublic 30 Days 30 Questions
- Join CTA Trailblazer Community study group
- Run 5+ full 2-hour mock board presentations
- Master all 8 Decision Guides (verbal articulation)
- Practice architecture diagramming by hand (< 10 min per diagram)
- Book CTA written exam slot

| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Sit CTA written exam; begin 30 Days 30 Questions challenge |
| 2 | CTA-601 workshop participation |
| 3 | Mock board session 1 + 2 with peers/community |
| 4 | Mock board session 3 + 4; review panel feedback; book board review slot |
