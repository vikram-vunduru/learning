# Salesforce Platform App Builder Certification — Course Overview

## About This Course

The Salesforce Platform App Builder certification (CRT-403) validates your ability to design, build, and deploy custom applications on the Salesforce Platform using declarative tools. This course is for professionals who need to move beyond administering Salesforce and start **building custom solutions** — creating objects, designing data models, automating business logic with Flow, building Lightning pages, and deploying apps across environments.

**Who this is for:**
- Salesforce Administrators ready to advance to app building
- Business Analysts who want to build solutions directly in Salesforce
- Developers seeking formal recognition of their declarative skills
- Anyone targeting a Salesforce developer or architect career path

**Prerequisite knowledge:** This course assumes familiarity with Salesforce fundamentals (org navigation, standard objects, basic administration). Completing the Salesforce Administrator certification (Course 02) or equivalent experience is strongly recommended before starting here.

## How This Differs from the Admin Certification

The Admin cert and the App Builder cert overlap on fundamentals, but they test **different skills**:

| Dimension | Admin (CRT-101) | App Builder (CRT-403) |
|---|---|---|
| Primary focus | Configuring and managing Salesforce | Designing and building custom apps |
| Data emphasis | Using standard objects | Creating custom objects and data models |
| Automation depth | Basic Flow, approval processes | Complex Flow, design patterns, when to automate |
| UI emphasis | Managing page layouts | Building Lightning pages, custom apps |
| Deployment | Not covered deeply | Change sets, sandbox strategy, deployment lifecycle |
| Mindset | "How do I manage this org?" | "How do I build this solution?" |

**Key difference:** App Builder tests your ability to *design* — choose the right tool, choose the right relationship type, choose the right security model for a new app. The exam is heavily scenario-based and tests judgment, not just memorization.

## Study Timeline

This is a **4-week study plan** designed for learners who already hold or have equivalent knowledge of the Salesforce Administrator certification.

### Week 1 — Foundations and Data Modeling
- **Days 1–2:** Complete Section 01 (Platform Fundamentals, L01–L04)
- **Days 3–5:** Complete Section 02 (Data Modeling, L05–L09)
- **Weekend:** Build a practice data model in a Developer Edition org — create custom objects, set up a Master-Detail and a Lookup relationship, use Schema Builder to review it

### Week 2 — Business Logic & Automation
- **Days 1–5:** Complete Section 03 (Business Logic, L10–L15)
- **Weekend:** Build a multi-step Flow in your Dev org — record-triggered flow that updates a field, sends a notification, and creates a child record

### Week 3 — User Interface & Deployment
- **Days 1–3:** Complete Section 04 (User Interface, L16–L20)
- **Days 4–5:** Complete Section 05 (App Deployment, L21–L23)
- **Weekend:** Build a Lightning App with a custom Lightning page, test a change set between two sandboxes

### Week 4 — Exam Prep
- **Days 1–2:** Review all EXAM TIPS sections in every lecture
- **Days 3–4:** Complete all practice exams in the exam-prep folder
- **Day 5:** Review weak areas identified by practice exams
- **Weekend:** Final read-through of key topics (relationships, Flow, security model)

**Target:** Schedule your exam for the end of Week 4 or start of Week 5.

**Q1:** Which exam domain has the highest weight on the CRT-403 Platform App Builder exam?
- A) Data Modeling & Management
- B) Salesforce Fundamentals
- C) Business Logic & Process Automation
- D) User Interface

**Answer:** C — Business Logic & Process Automation carries 28% of the exam weight, making it the single most heavily tested domain.

**Q2:** A Salesforce Admin wants to build a custom app for a field service team. The app needs custom objects, complex automations, and a custom Lightning page. Which certification most directly validates the skills needed to build this app?
- A) Salesforce Administrator
- B) Salesforce Platform App Builder
- C) Salesforce Service Cloud Consultant
- D) Salesforce Platform Developer I

**Answer:** B — The Platform App Builder certification specifically covers designing and building custom apps using declarative tools, including custom objects, automation, and Lightning pages.

**Q3:** How many questions are on the CRT-403 exam, and what is the passing score?
- A) 60 questions, 65% passing
- B) 65 questions, 63% passing
- C) 60 questions, 63% passing
- D) 60 questions, 70% passing

**Answer:** C — The CRT-403 exam has 60 scored questions with a passing score of 63% (approximately 38 correct answers required).
